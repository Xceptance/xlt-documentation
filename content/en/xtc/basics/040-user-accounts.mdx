## Creating a user account

To use Xceptance Test Center, you need a user account. Sign up at [https://xtc.xceptance.com/](https://xtc.xceptance.com/) by providing your name, email address, and password. Alternatively, [sign up using a Google or Microsoft account](#sign-up-using-an-external-provider).

:::note[Password Guidelines]

1. Your password for XTC must contain at least 12 characters.
1. Passwords must not start or end with spaces.
1. If shorter than 15 characters, the password must contain lowercase letters, uppercase letters, digits, and special characters (such as punctuation).
:::

After registering, check your inbox for an XTC confirmation email. Confirm the link to activate your account and sign in.

Xceptance assigns initial user roles and project permissions.

## Account management

To manage your account details, click your avatar on the right side of the header and select account settings.

The account settings view includes the _Profile_ and _Security_ tabs.

In **Profile**, update personal information such as your name, phone number (used for [monitoring notifications](/xtc/monitoring/scenarios/#notifications)), and avatar image. You can also [pause notifications](#pausing-notifications) here.

In **Security**, update authentication credentials. To change your email address or password, enter the new values twice, and confirm with your current password. If you authenticate through an external provider without a local XTC password, you can update your email address directly without providing a password.

You can also configure time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) as a [second factor](#two-factor-authentication).

Connected external login providers appear in this section. You can also [connect an existing XTC account to an external login provider](#connecting-existing-xtc-accounts-to-an-external-login-provider).

Organization administrators can enforce **mandatory login requirements** on members, such as specific login providers or 2FA.

## Brute-force protection

To prevent brute-force attacks on user accounts, XTC automatically locks a local user account for one hour after six consecutive unsuccessful login attempts. 

During the lockout period:

* Any login attempt for this account fails immediately, even with the correct password.
* For security reasons, XTC does not send a notification email about the lockout.
* If you require immediate access, an [Organization Administrator](/xtc/basics/organizations/#locking-members-of-an-organization) can unlock your account manually from the organization member list.

## Notifications

XTC notifies users by email whenever their membership changes:

* when added to or removed from an [organization](/xtc/basics/organizations/#adding-members-to-an-organization) or [project](/xtc/basics/projects/#adding-and-removing-project-members),
* when their [organization](/xtc/basics/organizations/#user-roles-within-an-organization) or [project](/xtc/basics/projects/#user-roles-within-a-project) role changes, or
* when an administrator [locks or unlocks](/xtc/basics/organizations/#locking-members-of-an-organization) their account.

### Pausing notifications

You can pause specific notifications for a defined period:

* Emails about project membership updates.
* Emails and SMS text messages for [monitoring alerts](/xtc/monitoring/scenarios/#notifications).

Security-related account notifications remain active at all times.

To pause notifications, click your avatar in the top right, open account settings, click the pencil icon next to _Notifications_, choose a pause duration, and click _Set_. The configured pause appears in the notifications panel:

![](/images/xtc/notifications_paused.png)

To delete a scheduled pause, click _Delete_ next to the _Edit_ button.

During an active pause, XTC suspends notifications. When the pause period ends, XTC resumes sending alerts automatically. Muted notifications are not resent. You do not need to delete an expired pause.

You might still receive monitoring alerts under the following conditions:

* Alerts sent to Slack channels. Mute the channel directly in Slack.
* Alerts delivered through external mailing lists. Unsubscribe or adjust mailbox filter rules.
* Addresses configured as [custom subscribers](/xtc/monitoring/monitoring-configuration/#defining-a-notification-list). Switch them to [XTC user subscribers](/xtc/monitoring/monitoring-configuration/#defining-a-notification-list).

## Two-factor authentication

You can secure your XTC account with a second factor alongside a password. XTC supports time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) through authenticator apps such as Twilio Authy or Google Authenticator.

To **enable 2FA**, open account settings, and select _Security_. In the _Two-Factor Authentication_ section, select _Enable Two-Factor Authentication_ and follow the on-screen steps to configure your authenticator app. On future logins, enter your regular password followed by the one-time code generated by your app.

:::note
When using an [external login provider](#external-login-providers), XTC does not request a TOTP code because the external identity provider manages two-factor authentication.
:::

To turn 2FA off, select _Disable Two-Factor Authentication_ and confirm using your password and a valid code from your authenticator app (or a recovery code).

XTC system administrators must enable 2FA for their local account.

### Two-factor recovery codes

You can generate fresh recovery codes at any time if you misplace previous codes. Navigate to account settings under _Security_ > _Two-Factor Authentication_, click _Reset Recovery Codes_, and follow the prompts. Resetting recovery codes does not affect your active authenticator app setup.

## External login providers

XTC supports single sign-on through Google or Microsoft accounts, enabling users to log in without managing separate credentials.

### Sign up using an external provider

To sign up using an external provider, click the Google or Microsoft button in the registration form:

![Registration form with buttons for external login providers](/images/xtc/registration-form.png)
*Registration form with buttons for external login providers*

:::note
If you are already signed in to your Google Account or Microsoft account, the provider might redirect back to XTC immediately without displaying a login screen.
:::

Upon return, complete the signup form with your name and email address. You receive an activation email to verify the address before account activation.

![Registration details form pre-filled with data from the login provider](/images/xtc/registration-form-return.png)
*Registration details form pre-filled with data from the login provider*

:::note
The form pre-populates details provided by your identity provider. You can adjust these values. XTC uses the email address for system notifications, including test reports.
:::

:::warning
The email address must be unique. Do not use shared addresses or distribution lists.
:::

### Connecting existing XTC accounts to an external login provider

To connect an existing account to an external provider:

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner and open account settings under _Security_.
2. Click the pencil icon in the _Single Sign-On_ section.
3. Choose your preferred login provider.

   ![UI to connect existing accounts to external providers.](/images/xtc/switch-to-external-login.png)
   *UI to connect existing accounts to external providers. The Google button appears inactive when already linked.*

   XTC redirects to your provider for authentication, then links the accounts upon return.

:::note
If you are already signed in to your Google Account or Microsoft account, the provider might redirect back immediately without displaying an interface.
:::

:::note
Connecting an external account preserves your existing XTC password. You can sign in using either authentication method.
:::

### Login using an external provider

To sign in using an external provider:

![Login form with buttons for external login providers](/images/xtc/login-form.png)
*Login form with buttons for external login providers*

Click the respective provider button on the login form to authenticate through Google or Microsoft.

### Removing the binding to an external login provider

To disconnect an external login provider and return to standard password authentication, your account must have a local password configured.

:::note
Users who registered through an external provider might not have a local password. Use the _Forgot Your Password?_ link on the login page to set one up.
:::

To remove an external binding:

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner and open account settings under _Security_.
2. Click the pencil icon in the _Single Sign-On_ section.
3. Select _Remove External Login Provider_ and enter your password to confirm.
