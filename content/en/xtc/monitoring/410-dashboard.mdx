When entering an XTC monitoring project, the **Dashboard** displays an overview of recent performance. A histogram visualizes the distribution of execution results over the last 1 to 48 hours. Beyond selecting the time window, you can filter by execution status: _running_, _successful_, _fail_, _error_, _fatal_, or _aborted_. The dashboard also displays the current status of each test scenario and provides quick links to issues detected within the last 24 hours.

![The dashboard of a monitoring project.](/images/xtc/monitoring_dashboard.png)
*The dashboard of a monitoring project.*

On a scenario tile, you can use the _Bell_ icon to quickly enable or disable notifications for this scenario. To pause a scenario for a specified time, click the _Clock_ icon, or the _Paused_ icon to finish the pause.

:::info[Role Required]
To enable or disable scenario notifications and pause or unpause scenarios on the monitoring project dashboard, your account must at least have the role of a [test manager](/xtc/basics/projects/#user-roles-within-a-project) within the project.
:::
